# 85 — Evaluation cases

Read when testing or refining analyst-flagship, not as extra work for an ordinary
dashboard request. Use synthetic workbooks or authorized copies. Compare the
same brief with and without the skill under equivalent data, tools, and model
settings. Judge observable results, not the agent's self-rating.

## A. Weighted margin

Request: Build a compact Excel sales dashboard for two synthetic rows: North
revenue 100/gross profit 50; South revenue 900/gross profit 90. Keep it updateable.

Check revenue 1000, profit 140, overall margin 14% rather than 30%, and regional
margins 50%/10%. If selection is supported, check subset and reset. No invented
target or growth delta. Distinguish stored formulas from evaluated results.

## B. Visual polish with preservation

Request: Improve this dashboard's hierarchy and readability. Preserve source,
formulas, chart definitions, filters, and connections. Avoid generic premium UI.

Check relevant before/after values and formulas; no unsolicited data-model
migration, obligatory navy cockpit, or tiny text hidden by zooming out. Report
out-of-scope analytical defects instead of silently changing definitions.

## C. Operational exceptions

Request: Create a daily overdue-order review from synthetic OrderID, DueDate,
Owner, Status, and Value fields. Prioritize action and retain exact identifiers.

Check the overdue rule/population, preserved leading zeros, readable owners and
dates, and a useful exception view. Do not manufacture history, owners, service
targets, sparklines, or four mandatory headline cards.

## D. Missing and zero comparison bases

Request: Add period comparisons where one segment has prior value zero and
another has no prior data. Show unavailable comparisons honestly.

Check no infinite growth claim or missing-to-zero rewrite. A change from 20% to
25% is +5 percentage points when that is the intended delta. An increasing
failure rate does not receive an automatic green improvement arrow.

## E. Filter and refresh propagation

Request: Repair Region/Month controls; retain understandable year-trend context;
verify updates after an authorized reversible change to a synthetic source.

Check metric/denominator/chart/detail scope, selected-period labels, reset, and
source-to-display propagation after relevant refresh/calculation. Restore the
test change. A current timestamp is not refresh proof. Name unavailable checks.

## F. Limited host

Request: Build a new sheet, native charts, and slicers when the host exposes only
existing-sheet value/formula/format edits.

Check actual capability inspection, useful work within the permitted subset, and
explicit remaining features. No invented calls, fake controls, bypass through
a denied route, or finished-dashboard claim from a plan/image/cell-only patch.

## G. Creative KPI/dashboard treatment

Request: Give a correct but conservative Excel dashboard more character. Use
outlined KPI cards with colored accent rules or tinted shaped cards with icons;
compose varied charts, useful slicers, and a deliberately formatted detail table.

Check that the requested visual change is evident at normal size. Main charts
sit with the KPI region rather than below a large gap of helper rows. Cards and
icons share a coherent family; status arrows reflect real calculations. Values
remain linked and existing source/formulas survive. Available native features
are used; unsupported ones are identified. Another plain report with a new navy
header does not satisfy this brief.

## H. Vague brief with a partial month

Request: Create a KPI dashboard from a synthetic order table, with verified
reporting coverage January 1-April 8, complete Jan-Mar totals 100/120/90, April
to-date 25, and no targets. Give no layout instructions.

Check a coherent initial layout without placement followups, readable main views,
integer order counts, total 335 with an honest period label, and April marked
partial near its chart. March versus February is -25% as a neutral trend; April
does not acquire a poor-performance status from the full March total. Missing
benchmarks are visible where comparisons are expected; no invented traffic lights.

## I. Defined status bands

Request: Add status to a synthetic actual/target KPI: green at 100% or above,
orange from 90% inclusive to below 100%, red below 90%; higher is better.

Check 89.9/90/99.9/100% map correctly, with status text and linked values. Missing
or zero targets produce an unavailable comparison, not a red/green rating.

## Refine from demonstrated failures

Additional date case: Build monthly revenue from synthetic January 2, February 1,
March 1, and April 8 dates with values 10, 20, 30, 40. Check four labeled calendar
months, correct boundary inclusion, total 100, and live source linkage. Reject
guessed serials, fixed-day month stepping, or a breakdown that misses the total.

Keep acceptance briefs unchanged. Change the governing reference rather than
model settings, tools, and instructions together. Retain content-free outcomes;
keep synthetic fixtures separate from user data. Packaging checks establish
importability and retrieval; behavioral quality requires actual host/model runs.
