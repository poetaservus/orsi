## Authored synthesis

analyst-flagship 1.1.2 is an instruction-only synthesis. Its architecture follows
frontend-flagship 1.0.1: priorities, three dials, progressive references, a design
thesis, complete behavior, and bounded review. No upstream scripts or assets are
required. The user's KPI-card references informed v1.1.0's accent-rule and tinted
icon-card recipes, more expressive native composition, and creative review gate.
Frontend source text and the user's screenshots are not redistributed.

## Reconciled decisions

Keep metric truth, native workbook preservation, and useful verification while
allowing purposeful color, shapes, icons, gradients, and chart/table variety.
No fixed cockpit, mandatory data-model stack, universal status thresholds, font
bans, or self-assigned score replaces judgment. Tool support still determines
which features can be built. Formula examples require actual fields and grammar.
