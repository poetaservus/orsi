# 80 — Review, defects, and evidence

Read before completing substantial creation, redesign, or repair. For review-only
work, report defects without editing. Scale review to scope and document length.

## Content and structure

Compare required sections, exact phrases, figures, units, dates, qualifications,
and source links against the supplied material. Look for omissions, duplication,
stale placeholders, changed meaning, and invented claims. Check semantic headings,
lists, tables, captions, fields, and cross-references as applicable. A text extract
can support content checks but cannot establish pagination or visual quality.

For existing files, compare the relevant before/after structures and properties.
Verify that intentional emphasis and out-of-scope content survived. Pay special
attention to the features the chosen editing route could drop. A successful
open does not prove preservation of comments, bookmarks, or revision history.

## Actual rendered pages

Inspect the saved output's rendering when available, at thumbnail and reading
size. Check all pages for ordinary-length deliverables. For very long work,
combine a whole-file overview with readable risk/representative pages and state
coverage. Inspect the opening, interior flow, transitions, tables, and ending.

Collect defects across hierarchy, font substitution/glyphs, line measure,
spacing, awkward breaks, blank pages, table overflow, image quality, captions,
headers/footers, numbering, and contrast. Confirm that the requested creative
treatment is visible and coherent. Do not approve the first page while ignoring
the body or hide cramped text by viewing only thumbnails.

Apply the [20](20-composition-and-taste.md) taste checks: generic blue report
defaults and premium navy/gold defaults can both fail. Remove unsupported
ornament, improve page rhythm, and preserve purposeful expressive choices.
Do not use a self-awarded quality score as evidence of good design.

## Bounded corrections

Perform one broad inspection, a coherent batch of fixes, then confirmation of
changed pages and downstream reflow. Continue for observed blocking defects.
Do not re-render indefinitely to pursue imaginary perfection. If the renderer
is unavailable, complete structural checks and state that visual QA remains open.

## Keep evidence categories distinct

| Evidence | Establishes | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Package/schema validation | The checks performed on document structure | Content truth, readable layout |
| Text/structure readback | Observed content and native roles | Rendered typography or page flow |
| Rendered page inspection | Layout in that renderer and inspected coverage | Native editability or universal compatibility |
| Field update plus readback | Observed refreshed field results | Every uninspected reference |
| Save plus reopen/readback | Persisted result checked | Preservation of every unexamined feature |

Report the artifact, material changes, real checks, and remaining limits. A
host-limited patch is a supported partial edit; a plan is a plan. Neither is
proof of a finished typeset Word document.
