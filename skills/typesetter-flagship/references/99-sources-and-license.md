Read for provenance or redistribution. Notices live inside the supported

## Authored synthesis

typesetter-flagship 1.0.0 is an instruction-only synthesis researched on
16 October 2026. Its architecture follows the user's frontend-flagship 1.0.1
and analyst-flagship 1.1.2: priorities, three dials, progressive references,
a design contract, complete implementation, and bounded verification. Word
guidance adds semantic styles, editable structures, preservation, pagination,
and evidence appropriate to the actual host.

The package contains original guidance, not a vendored document engine. No
upstream executable, image, font, template, customer document, or proprietary
skill text is redistributed. Upstream tools mentioned in research are not
dependencies or assumed capabilities. Pinned public research snapshots and
inventories are kept outside this portable package.
